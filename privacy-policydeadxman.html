1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Max Battista(“I”, "me", "my") operating the Nature Compiler application ("the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you connect your TikTok account to the App.

The App is a personal tool used to create and publish nature video compilations to TikTok via TikTok's official Content Posting API. It is not a commercial service or platform offered to the general public.

3. What Information We Collect

3.1 TikTok Account Data

When you connect a TikTok account to the App via OAuth, we receive and temporarily store:

  • Your TikTok Open ID (a unique identifier assigned by TikTok)
  • An OAuth access token and refresh token, used to post on your behalf
  • Basic creator info (username, posting capabilities) retrieved from TikTok's API at the time of posting

We do not collect your TikTok password, personal profile information, follower data, or any content from your TikTok account beyond what is needed to publish videos.

3.2 Video Content

Video files processed by the App (nature compilation clips) are handled locally or on the server temporarily during upload to TikTok. They are deleted immediately after the upload completes. We do not store, analyse, or share your video content.

3.3 Usage Data

We may collect basic server logs (IP addresses, request timestamps, error messages) for the purposes of debugging and maintaining the App. These logs are not shared with third parties and are retained for a maximum of 30 days.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your TikTok account and maintain your connected session
  • To publish video compilations to your TikTok profile on your behalf
  • To refresh access tokens to maintain a continuous connection with TikTok's API
  • To diagnose technical issues with the App

The legal basis for processing under UK GDPR is legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) — specifically, the operation of a personal content publishing tool — and consent (Article 6(1)(a)) given at the point of TikTok OAuth authorisation.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data may be shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • TikTok: Content and metadata (caption, sound ID, privacy settings) are sent to TikTok via their official API when publishing. TikTok's own Privacy Policy governs how TikTok handles this data.
  • Hosting providers: If the App is hosted on a cloud platform (e.g. Railway, Render), your data may pass through their infrastructure. These providers act as data processors and are contractually bound to handle data securely.
  • Legal obligation: We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

6. Data Retention

  • OAuth tokens — stored in server memory or a local database and deleted when you disconnect your account or the session expires (tokens expire after 24 hours; refresh tokens after 365 days).
  • Video files — deleted immediately after upload to TikTok.
  • Server logs — retained for up to 30 days, then deleted.

7. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

As a UK resident, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — you can request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — you can request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — you can request deletion of your data at any time
  • Right to restrict processing — you can request that we limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability — you can request your data in a portable format
  • Right to withdraw consent — you can disconnect your TikTok account at any time via TikTok's app settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at deadxmanxdeadxman@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you believe your data has been handled unlawfully.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including storing OAuth tokens securely (not in plain text where possible), using HTTPS for all communications, and limiting access to the App's backend to authorised users only.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13, or under the minimum age required by TikTok's Terms of Service in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
